# SEO engagement questions

## Why can a live page remain absent from search?

A URL may be undiscovered, blocked, incorrectly rendered, treated as a duplicate or excluded for other reasons. Verify the specific evidence before changing it. A successful HTTP response and sitemap entry establish accessibility signals, not a guaranteed indexing outcome. Review the template and the page's standalone value together.

## Does a technical audit include implementation?

Only if the agreed scope includes it. A useful proposal separates diagnosis, deployment and verification, with owners for development and editorial changes. Ask whether the provider can test production output and what happens when another supplier must implement a recommendation. Findings need a retest boundary.

## Should every city receive a landing page?

Create a location page when it provides useful, distinct information about actual service delivery. Replacing a place name across otherwise identical pages creates little value. Do not invent offices, reviews or local results. A service-area business should also represent its real operating model in local profiles.

## Is E-E-A-T a score we can purchase or calculate?

No public numerical E-E-A-T score is supplied by Google. Accurate identity, trustworthy evidence, qualified review and useful content support readers' evaluation. A third-party dashboard may have its own score, but that should not be represented as Google's internal assessment.

## Can structured data create a review result?

Markup describes supported visible information and must meet the relevant feature policies. Valid syntax alone does not make every organisation eligible for a review enhancement. Never invent ratings or turn an unsupported testimonial into structured evidence. Keep product facts synchronised with the live catalogue.

## Can AI replace editorial judgement?

Models can assist with drafting, clustering and classification. They can also invent facts or propose incorrect technical advice. A responsible workflow verifies sources, records changes and gives an accountable person control over publication. Producing more pages without a maintenance plan can increase costs while reducing clarity.

## How quickly should we expect results?

Agree milestones for diagnosis, implementation and verification. Search outcomes depend on demand, competing pages and search-engine decisions; do not convert an implementation deadline into a ranking promise. Early reporting should show evidence of completed work and observed changes, including inconclusive findings.

## What information helps a project enquiry?

Share public URLs, platform, audience, important conversions and known changes. Summarise previous work and access constraints. Do not send customer exports, passwords or private analytics through a public issue.
